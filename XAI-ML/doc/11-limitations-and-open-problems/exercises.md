# Exercises — 11 Limitations & Open Problems

1. **Explanation ≠ justification.** Give an example where a model's explanation could be technically accurate ("the model used feature X") but ethically or legally problematic to rely on.
2. **Stability problem.** Referencing lesson 03 and 04, why do SHAP and LIME explanations sometimes disagree with each other, and why might even the *same* method give different results on a slightly different dataset sample?
3. **Human over-trust.** Design a small user study (in words) that could test whether showing an explanation makes people trust a wrong prediction more than they would with no explanation at all.
4. **Manipulation risk.** Sketch (conceptually, not code) how someone might craft an input that fools both a black-box model's prediction AND its SHAP/LIME explanation simultaneously, so the flawed prediction looks "well explained."
5. **Pick an open question.** Choose one of the five open research questions listed in the theory (Causal XAI, Interactive Explanations, Multi-objective Explanations, Explanation Verification, Scalable XAI) and write a short paragraph (3-5 sentences) proposing one concrete next step a researcher could take to make progress on it.
