# Exercises — 10 Modalities, Regulation & Ethics

1. **Modality mapping.** For each of the following, name the most appropriate XAI method from the theory: (a) explaining which words drove a sentiment classifier's decision, (b) explaining which region of a chest X-ray drove a pneumonia diagnosis, (c) explaining which past days' sales drove a demand forecast.
2. **GDPR Article 22.** List the four requirements GDPR imposes on automated decisions. Pick one (e.g. a loan-approval bot) and describe what output would satisfy all four.
3. **FDA tiers.** Explain why a "Tier 1 (High-risk)" medical AI system is restricted to glass-box models only, even though black-box models often have higher raw accuracy. What is the FDA implicitly prioritizing?
4. **Cross-modality connection.** Grad-CAM (images) and Attention Visualization (text) are both described as "highlighting" methods. What do they have in common structurally, and how do they differ in what they highlight (pixels vs. tokens)?
5. **Regulatory gap.** Pick one of the three frameworks (GDPR, FDA, Algorithmic Accountability Act) and identify a scenario or model type it doesn't clearly cover (e.g. a chatbot, a search ranking algorithm, an ad-targeting system). What explanation obligations, if any, should arguably apply there?
